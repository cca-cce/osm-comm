The reporting centre brings together our key
reports, policies and commitments, and our
sustainability data and performance, in addition to
reporting standards and frameworks.
Our annual sustainability reporting data is presented in the
Annual report, and our sustainability statement is prepared
and presented in accordance with the Norwegian AccountingSearch ENNO Menu
Act, including implementation of the Corporate Sustainability
Reporting Directive (CSRD), and compliance with the European
Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS) and Article 8 of EU
Regulation 2020/852 (the “Taxonomy Regulation”).
The data set presented in Equinor Sustainability Data Hub
include the full scope of our ESG reporting and the data
presented is prepared and informed by the Global Reporting
Initiative (GRI) Standards.
